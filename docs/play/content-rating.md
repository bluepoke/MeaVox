# Content rating questionnaire for MeaVox

For the Play Console (App content → Content ratings, IARC questionnaire). MeaVox is a singing-practice
tool: the user brings their own songs, and the app has no social features, no purchases and no
content of its own besides the lyrics it looks up for the user's songs.

| Question | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Category | **Utility, Productivity, Communication, or Other** (not a game) | A music tool. |
| Violence, fear, sexuality, gambling, drugs, alcohol, tobacco | **No** | The app has no such content of its own. |
| Crude humour or profanity in the app's own content | **No** | Lyrics shown are those of the user's own songs, looked up at LRCLIB; the app adds none. |
| Does the app allow users to interact or exchange content with other users? | **No** | No accounts, chat or uploads; exports are shared only through Android's share sheet, by the user. |
| Does the app share the user's current physical location? | **No** | |
| Does the app allow users to purchase digital goods? | **No** | |
| Does the app contain ads? | **No** | |
| Is the app a web browser or search engine? | **No** | |
| Does the app contain unrestricted internet access or user-generated content? | **No** | It connects only to LRCLIB (lyrics) and GitHub (models). |

Expected result: rated for all ages (e.g. PEGI 3, USK 0, ESRB Everyone). Note that lyrics of the
user's songs may contain explicit words; they come from the user's own music, not from the app.
